HomeBase

A home-maintenance tracker built around a 3D model of your actual home. You don't fill out forms about your house — you build it, and the checklist writes itself. Appliances, systems, structural assets and vehicles each carry their own seasonal tasks, lifespan rating and upkeep status.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Includes a one-time in-app purchase to unlock premium features.

Age is not the same as upkeep

Every tracked item shows two badges that answer two different questions. A water heater can be well within its lifespan while its annual flush is a year overdue — our apps show you both, side by side.

ABC

Lifespan rating

How far into (or past) its expected service life the item is, computed from its install year — or your home's build year until you set one.

Upkeep status

Whether the item's recurring maintenance is current, overdue, or has never been done — driven by your actual "Mark Done" history, not the calendar's optimism.

A note on safety

Reminders are ours. Safe conditions are yours.

Many of the tasks our apps remind you about — garage doors, pool chemicals, vehicle fluids, electrical panels — involve real hazards, especially to children. Each usage guide includes plain-language child-safety warnings for every one of them (HomeBase, HomeHealth). Please read them before your first weekend of checklist-clearing.
